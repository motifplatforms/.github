# Motif Platforms

**AI + Data = Better Decisions.** An AI consultancy that works from strategy to production.

We help organizations move AI from slideware to working systems: strategy, transformation management and growth,
backed by engineers who build and evaluate the systems themselves.

### Open source

| Repo | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**mosaic-benchmarks**](https://github.com/motifplatforms/mosaic-benchmarks) | Headline results from Mosaic, our benchmark of agents on real business deliverables |
| [**aspera-grader-kit**](https://github.com/motifplatforms/aspera-grader-kit) | Grade the Word, PowerPoint and Excel files AI agents produce: inspectors, explainable checks, a JSON rubric and a worked example |

### What we build
- **Agent evaluation:** exercises, graders and benchmarks for knowledge-work AI
- **Decision platforms:** command centers and reporting that put data in front of the people deciding
- **Production web:** content-driven sites on Next.js, Sanity, Supabase and Vercel

Client engagements and internal tooling stay private by design. The open repos above are clean, standalone versions
of the patterns we use.
